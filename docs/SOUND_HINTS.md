# Sound hints

Reference for writing "sounds like" text (unit cards, `pron_tip`). The owner's ear is Midwest US (Iowa).

**Owner, 2026-11-17:** hints are approximate. Language varies with every mouth; the native recording carries the
real sound. Keep hints short and in plain words (an English example word, "a puff of air"), no phonetics terms
("buzzing", "voiced", "tense"). Do not keep re-asking a hint the owner has already agreed.

## Agreed anchors (taught units)
| Unit | Hint |
|---|---|
| ແ◌ (ແລ້ວ laew "already", ແກ້ວ gaew "glass") | e in "bed", held long, leaning towards the a in "say" |
| ◌ໍ, ◌ອ◌ (ບໍ່ bor "not", ຂໍ khor "may I have", ນອນ non "sleep") | "aw" with rounded lips, halfway to "oh" |
| short ◌ິ (ກິນ gin "eat", ດິນ din "earth", ຫິວ hiw "hungry") | a short "ee", shorter than the ee in "been". IPA /i/, not the /ɪ/ of "bin"; English ears may still hear "gin" because it is short. |
| ◌ຳ (ດຳ dam "black", ນ້ຳ nam "water") | the o in "Tom" (American), short; same vowel in both |
| ບ, ດ | the b in "boy", the d in "deck" (owner's wife) |
| final ◌ຍ (ສະບາຍດີ sabaidee "hello") | the y at the end of "bye" |
| ສະ in ສະບາຍດີ | ends in a short catch (R01); not always audible in speech, keep the hint |
| ຈ (ເຈົ້າ jao "you") | card says between the j in "jaw" and the ch in "chew"; owner leans to "j", open in `docs/QUESTIONS.md` |

The voiced/plain/puff three-way contrast is ບ/ປ/ພ and ດ/ຕ/ທ. It is the hardest one for English speakers, and the "not X" warnings above are its main defence. If one recap card for the trio is planned, it should repeat them.
